## Structure tags

Capture observable values for:

- hook at 1/3/5 seconds;
- problem or promise;
- conflict and reversal;
- first evidence timestamp;
- evidence type: bill, meter, switch test, installation, certification, third-party test, user testimony;
- person, product, and environment shown;
- number density and subtitle pace;
- trust component;
- comment trigger;
- product-reveal timestamp;
- CTA and destination.

## Replicability status

- `single_example`: one candidate only.
- `emerging_pattern`: two comparable candidates.
- `reusable_pattern`: at least three comparable candidates with evidence across accounts or markets.
- `validated_pattern`: reused by Wotai content and supported by downstream results.

## Business relevance

Rate separately from virality:

- `need_fit`: ties to a real market need.
- `product_fit`: can demonstrate a supported product capability.
- `evidence_fit`: Wotai can produce the proof without misleading claims.
- `channel_fit`: matches target platform format.
- `conversion_fit`: has a plausible route to dealer, installer, landing page, or lead form.
