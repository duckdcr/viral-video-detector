# Compliance policy

Use this reference for data handling and for candidates involving competitor, certification, subsidy, safety, savings, or payback claims. This is an operational checklist, not legal advice.

## Data handling

- Collect the minimum public fields needed for discovery, metrics, evidence, and audit.
- Record source, collection time, route, retention class, and deletion obligations.
- Store comment text only when required for analysis; prefer aggregates or short evidence snippets.
- Keep credentials in the approved secret store and redact them from logs.
- Treat full-video download, long-term retention, redistribution, and publication as separate permissions.
- Apply contractual deletion, retention, cross-border, and data-subject handling requirements.

## Content review triggers

Require human final review when a candidate or adaptation includes:

- named competitor comparison;
- certification or regulatory status;
- battery safety, fire, disaster, or emergency claims;
- subsidy, rebate, tax-credit, or eligibility guidance;
- electricity savings, payback period, return, or guaranteed performance;
- customer testimony whose permission or typicality is unclear;
- AI-generated or sponsored content disclosure requirements.

## Evidence contract

For each claim, retain:

- exact claim text;
- target market and validity date;
- source URL or internal approved evidence;
- evidence owner;
- review status and reviewer;
- expiry or recheck date where policy, tariff, or subsidy can change.

## Candidate labels

- `clear_for_ideation`: structure may enter the idea pool; factual claims still require validation during production.
- `needs_compliance_review`: candidate is useful but contains controlled claims.
- `research_only`: retain for trend understanding; do not adapt into marketing.
- `blocked`: do not use; preserve only the minimal audit record.

## Agent behavior

Separate source facts from interpretation. Phrase uncertain market fit as a hypothesis. Surface missing evidence and the next reviewer; do not smooth over gaps with generic disclaimers.
